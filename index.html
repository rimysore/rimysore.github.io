What makes it hold up
Real rule-engine fidelity

12 WAFv2 statement types modeled with AWS's exact JSON one-of shape, evaluated in priority order with correct short-circuit and cross-rule label propagation.

Guardrails in code, not trust

Every rule stages in COUNT mode, replays against legit traffic with deliberate false-positive bait, and an independent check auto-rolls-back any rule that regresses.

Capacity-constrained defense

A deliberately tight WCU budget forces the defender to consolidate and retire rules rather than stack regex — mirroring real operational limits.

Backend-agnostic runtime

A hand-rolled tool-calling loop — no LangChain or CrewAI — swaps between local Ollama, a deterministic CI backend, or a hosted LLM with zero agent-logic changes.
